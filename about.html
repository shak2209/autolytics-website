About Me

Shakeel Malik, founder of Autolytics IT Consultancy Ltd

Hello, I'm Shakeel

I'm an ISTQB-qualified, Certified Agile Tester with more than 13 years of software testing experience. I've worked across front end, middleware and back end systems, and I've progressed from Test Analyst to Test Lead, Test Delivery Lead and Test Manager.

I set up Autolytics IT Consultancy to give organisations practical, senior testing support. I write the test strategy and lead the team, but I'm just as comfortable doing the testing myself: API tests in Postman, SQL against the database, or building a Playwright automation framework.

I believe in shift-left testing. When I receive business requirements I run Three Amigos sessions with the BA and developers to clarify questions and close gaps, so test scenarios are ready before development begins. Problems found early are the cheapest ones to fix.

How I work

  • Communication: I work comfortably with project managers, business analysts, developers, testers, key stakeholders and end users, and I can explain technical detail at whatever level is needed.
  • Teamwork: I know my part affects the success of the wider delivery. Outside work I sit on the committee of my local football club.
  • Problem solving: I narrow down faults until I find the real cause. If I raise a problem, I also bring a proposed solution.
